Problem Statement:

Existing food platforms cause severe decision fatigue by relying on endless menus and generic cuisine filters, ignoring the vital link between emotion and appetite. When users crave comfort, celebration or quick boost, scrolling through hundreds of irrelevant listings leads to frustration rather than satisfaction. Moodmeal solves this disconnect with an emotion-first discovery experience, instantly translating current feelings into curated meal and restaurant recommendations.

Project Description

A food discovery platform where users input their current mood and get personalised restaurant or recipe recommendations.
